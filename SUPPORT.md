# Support

- **Your server or account on cubepals.com:** write to **support@cubepals.com**. Issues here are
  for blocklyd's code, and the people who can look into your account read the mailbox.
- **A bug in blocklyd, or an idea:** [open an issue](https://github.com/cubepals/blocklyd/issues/new/choose).
- **A security problem:** report it privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says.
- **Running your own fleet:** Cubepals'
  [fleet operations guide](https://github.com/cubepals/cubepals/blob/main/docs/fleet-operations.md).
